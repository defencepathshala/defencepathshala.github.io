Privacy Policy

Defence Pathshala Automation · Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how Defence Pathshala Automation ("the app"), an internal tool operated by Defence Pathshala, handles data obtained through Google APIs.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the Defence Pathshala team to manage our own YouTube channel (@DefencePathshala74). It is not available to the public.

Data we access

When our team signs in with Google, the app requests access to:

  • YouTube upload: to upload and schedule videos to our own channel
  • YouTube account management: to update our own videos and reply to comments on them
  • YouTube Analytics (read-only): to read our own channel's performance reports

When the app reads comments on our videos, it may process the public comment text and the commenter's public display name only to prepare a reply.

How data is used

Data is used only to publish and manage content on our own channel and to plan our content. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

The Google sign-in token is stored only on our own computer. Analytics data may be kept in our private reports. We do not run a public server that stores user data.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Google's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.
