Defence Pathshala Automation

Internal publishing tool for the Defence Pathshala YouTube channel

Defence Pathshala creates free preparation content for the NDA (National Defence Academy) entrance exam: maths tricks, previous-year questions, exam updates and stories from NDA life.

Defence Pathshala Automation is a private, internal tool used only by the Defence Pathshala team to manage our own YouTube channel, @DefencePathshala74. It uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to:

  • Upload and schedule videos that our team has produced, on our own channel
  • Reply to comments on our own videos
  • Read our own channel's analytics to plan upload times and topics

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's channel or data.
